Detailed Description

DHT11 temperature and humidity sensor on a single data pin.

The protocol needs an 18 ms start pulse followed by a 4 ms bit stream. To avoid a busy wait of 18 ms, reading is split: dht11_startRequest pulls the line low, the caller waits at least 18 ms (rtos_delay), then dht11_readResponse releases the line and decodes the bits. The decoding busy waits for about 4 ms with interrupts enabled; pulse widths are measured by polling, with a wide margin between the 26 us "0" and 70 us "1" pulses. A tick interrupt landing in a pulse can stretch it by about 10 us, which is harmless.
